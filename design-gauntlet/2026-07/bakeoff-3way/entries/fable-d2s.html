How It Works

The Staged to Sell System

Nineteen-plus years of staging Atlanta homes, distilled into three deliberate steps.

  1. 01

    The Walkthrough

    Barbara and her team walk your home the way a buyer will — room by room, sight line by sight line. You get a clear staging plan that says exactly what each space needs, and nothing it doesn’t.

  2. 02

    The Staging

    Furniture, art, light, and styling, placed by a team that has staged thousands of homes. Every room is composed for the camera first, because buyers meet your listing online before they ever stand in it.

  3. 03

    The Sale

    Your listing goes live photo-ready, and the staging stays in place through showings — so every visit, from first click to final walkthrough, meets the same composed home.
