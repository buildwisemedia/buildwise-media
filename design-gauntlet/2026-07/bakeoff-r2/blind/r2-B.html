03 · The punchline

Two kinds of capacity. Two different levers.

Flat-rate seat · prepaid capacity
The lever is utilization.

A flat seat costs the same whether it runs 7 tasks or 719. July’s runs moved the bill by $0.00 — which means every idle hour is capacity you already paid for, going to waste. Feed the seat.

JULY · 719 runs · $0.00 marginal each
Metered API · elastic capacity
The lever is quality per dollar.

On the meter, spend follows value token by token. $1.19 bought a complete 3-model blind bake-off — fund small, judge the output blind, then scale only what wins.

KIMI K3 · $25.00 funded → $1.19 spent · $23.81 unspent
04 · Acting on it

Three moves for next month.

Keep code on the flat seat.

— of all runs are multi-file code edits at $0.00 marginal. Prepaid capacity is doing exactly what it was bought for — don’t route that work anywhere priced per token.

Point the seat at design next.

Design builds are the heaviest tasks on the books — median 24.7 min — yet only 5 ran all July. On a flat plan, more of them are free at the margin. That’s unused leverage.

Buy experiments on the meter.

A full blind bake-off — 6 pages + 6 scorecards — cost $1.19 all-in, with $23.81 of the funded $25.00 still unspent. Small metered bets are the cheapest way to judge a new model.
