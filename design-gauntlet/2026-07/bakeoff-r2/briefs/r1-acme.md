# Design Dispatch Brief — ROUND 1: ACME Heating & Air, Homepage. BRING THE WOW.

One-pass build. There is no follow-up round. This is a design competition entry: memorable wins.

## Output contract
- ONE complete, self-contained HTML file. All CSS in one inline `<style>` block; vanilla JS inline as needed. The ONLY external requests allowed are Google Fonts `<link>` tags. No CDNs, no external images, no frameworks.
- ACME Heating & Air is a FICTIONAL company (premium residential HVAC, Metro Atlanta). Unlike a real-client build, you MAY invent believable content this round: services, guarantees, membership pricing, stats, review quotes with first-name-only attributions. Keep it plausible for a real premium HVAC operator — this page is judged as if a real owner would ship it.
- Still banned: fake photographs (photo areas = art-directed placeholder surfaces in your palette with a small caption of the intended shot), real review-platform logos/badges, real brands' names or marks.
- Fully responsive 320px → 1440px+. Semantic HTML, accessible contrast, `tel:` links on phone numbers.

## The round
"Bring the wow." Full creative freedom: any palette, any type system, any layout language, any graphics (inline SVG art, patterns, generative CSS, scroll motion, micro-interactions). Light or dark. The judges are a business owner and rival design teams — the page must be arresting in the first second AND credible enough that a homeowner with a broken AC at 9pm converts.

## What the page must DO (jobs, not layout prescriptions)
- Emergency path: 24/7 phone visible fast, click-to-call.
- Booking path: schedule a tune-up / estimate.
- Services: cooling, heating, air quality, maintenance membership (invent a good name and structure).
- Trust: reviews, guarantees, licensing/insured, years serving, financing.
- A memorable brand moment for "ACME" — make the name feel premium, not cartoon.
- Footer with service areas + hours.

## Hard bans (generic-AI-slop devices — wow that isn't these)
1. Rounded/geometric display sans at weight ≥800 as your display voice
2. The default saturated coral/red accent (hue 0–25°) as your accent choice
3. Decorative half-circle arc clutter (border-radius:50% motifs)
4. Rotated sticker/stamp/badge devices
5. Ghost numerals (huge type at opacity ≤ 0.25)
6. Number-in-a-circle step cards
7. Autoplay media, aggressive popups

## Craft bar
Judged blind against two rival builds of this same brief on: first-second impact, hierarchy, typographic craft, color discipline, distinctiveness, and conversion credibility for the 9pm-broken-AC visitor.
