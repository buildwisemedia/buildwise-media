# BWM Design Department (peer contract v1.1)

This contract binds every artifact whose quality depends on visual, verbal,
interaction, or information-design judgment. It improves process without
turning BWM taste, a model's house style, or one client's trade dress into a
universal aesthetic.

## Authority

- Robert + Sol originate and calibrate BWM creative direction. Client identity
  and client approval govern client-owned production work.
- Fable is an independent architecture, truth, usability, accessibility, and
  acceptance critic. Fable may also author a sealed blind candidate, but may
  never provide the binding score for its own work.
- No author family supplies the only binding judgment on its own artifact.
- Brain `reference/Design-Identity-Resolver.md` owns identity and inheritance.
- Brain `specs/Spec-Art-Direction-and-Information-Design.md` owns the durable
  process. The machine contract is `bwm-design-run/1.0.0` in the installed
  module registry and schema; all authors, scorers, gates, and blind reviews
  consume that exact version.
- When a legacy surface skill conflicts with this contract, this contract wins
  on identity isolation, client-specific action language, evidence,
  independence, and premium/production claims unless a newer locked client
  source explicitly says otherwise.

## Intake and process tier

Before authoring, declare the surface module and one tier:

- `full`: flagship, new identity direction, material redesign, or new product;
- `slim`: extension of an approved system with bounded novelty;
- `locked-direction-implementation`: deterministic execution of an approved
  direction without reopening taste;
- `taste-lab`: neutral/internal exploration; never client approval or ship
  evidence.

Resolve identity, freeze the truth/content/data packet, and record source and
asset rights. Missing, conflicting, low-confidence, or rights-unknown inputs
block brand projection. A taste lab may continue only when labeled local,
synthetic/neutral, and non-production.

Website preflight composes with this contract: neutral single-file pages on
internal, access-gated review surfaces may use the v4 marker's
`artifact_mode: internal-neutral` (identity record + source manifest bindings
only, `ndb_schema: none`; hash-bound and creator-stamped as always). The mode
is locked to `identity_class: neutral` + `single` composition — never a
lighter gate for client, BWM, or HRE surfaces (2026-08-03).

## Surface modules

Choose exactly one primary module. Add secondary-module checks when needed.

1. `website_landing`
2. `dashboard_app`
3. `data_visualization`
4. `deck_report_proposal`
5. `sales_ux_marketing_copy`
6. `campaign_email_social`
7. `review_decision_artifact`

Copy is also cross-cutting: every surface must connect audience, problem,
specific proof, decision sequence, and action language in the resolved voice.
Copy polish cannot repair weak information architecture or invented proof.

## Full-loop process

1. Resolve identity and prohibited inheritance.
2. Freeze source truth, scope, data, proof, and asset provenance.
3. Research 16–26 current sources for a new direction; preserve dates, URLs,
   transferable relationships, and non-transferable trade dress.
4. Build the decision/story model before styling.
5. Author 2–3 materially distinct, evidenced directions when the decision is
   genuinely open. Do not re-compete an approved direction.
6. Build the highest-risk slice first: the interaction, hierarchy, chart,
   narrative, or copy decision most likely to invalidate the direction.
7. Run at most three render/inspect/fix loops unless a written exception names
   the new fact being tested.
8. Emit `design-run.json` and a human-readable receipt.
9. Run deterministic module QA and `bwm-design-gate run design-run.json`.
10. Obtain fresh-context opposite-family review and an open-ended defect pass.
11. Obtain the required Robert/client HIL decision.
12. Preserve exact local/review/staged/production state and capture learning.

Slim and locked-direction tiers may reduce research and options, but never
remove identity, truth, provenance, deterministic QA, state truth, or required
HIL. Record what was inherited and why reopening direction was unnecessary.

## Premium craft ledger (noncompensatory)

Art direction, typography, composition, motion restraint, and storytelling/
information design are mandatory craft accounts on every governed surface.
Each dimension must record `intent`, `reference_relationship`, and
`surface_specific_anchor` (observable pass/fail cues for this module, identity,
audience, and job), plus `inspection_note`, cite real evidence, and score at
least 90 before the machine may nominate `premium-candidate`. The binding score
comes only from the declared independent, fresh-context scorer whose family is
different from the author family; an author's self-score is diagnostic only.
Strength elsewhere cannot compensate for a weak or unaccounted craft dimension.

Use the dated BWM gold-standard calibration set to judge commitment and craft,
not to copy surfaces: Bioeutectics (product-led commercial clarity), Cedimba
(editorial/clinical restraint), Eywa (cinematic category authority), Biocle
(soft luxury and memorable metaphor), Antarka (dark scientific authority and
action), and Acquisition.com (direct-response clarity, proof density, offer
architecture, and conversion discipline), Pentagram (identity-system depth,
typographic range, and case-study framing), and Clay (digital-product craft,
interaction, motion, and portfolio storytelling), Turno EV (cinematic technical
storytelling, category problem framing, and buyer-path clarity), and Everything
Design (editorial B2B positioning, sequenced proof, conversion architecture,
and human-first answer structure), and FuseLab Creative (enterprise dashboard
hierarchy, AI-interface decision support, and data-product information design).
Extract relationships only. Every
direction must choose an identity-native register and execute it coherently to
the ceiling; editorial moves are not a substitute for category legibility or
commercial action.

For `website_landing`, premium craft and website effectiveness are parallel
noncompensatory gates. Required evidence separately proves a performance
budget, technical SEO, AEO answer surfaces, the primary conversion path, and
analytics instrumentation. Prelaunch evidence proves readiness and a
testable conversion architecture, not a conversion-rate promise; postlaunch
performance claims require observed analytics. Technical compliance cannot
excuse generic art direction, and visual excellence cannot excuse a slow,
unfindable, unanswerable, or unmeasurable experience.

For `dashboard_app`, required evidence separately proves decision hierarchy,
density legibility, data provenance, latency feedback, keyboard/mobile-priority
behavior, and all registered system states. When AI generates, recommends, or
acts, also expose uncertainty/confidence, provenance, failure boundaries,
reversibility, and the appropriate human control. “AI speed” never excuses
hidden state, inaccessible density, or action without accountable review.

## Evidence and judgment

Evidence classes are A (artifact/pixel/copy output), S (source, identity,
rights, truth, or provenance), I (interaction, responsive, accessibility,
performance, or operational evidence), and J (independent judgment, rationale,
defect, or HIL evidence). The module registry defines the required combination.
Dimension scores cite exact evidence locators; an unsupported score is invalid.

The weighted score is diagnostic. It may reject or nominate a
`premium-candidate`; it can never accept `premium`. Hard blockers always win.
An independent reviewer performs an open-ended pass and may promote an
unlisted defect to blocker status. Only the applicable human decision may
accept a judgment-dependent premium label.

Improvement claims require the same frozen content, scope, evidence area,
author family, and protocol. Otherwise call the result a new-line benchmark.

## Blind calibration

For before/after process tests, hold the author family constant within each
old-process/new-process pair. Randomize anonymous order, hide routes and scores,
seal Robert's choice before reveal, and preserve component-level synthesis.
Collect separate blind choices for art direction, typography, composition,
motion restraint, storytelling, information design, category/commercial
legibility, proof/trust, conversion clarity, and responsive/mobile usability;
split decisions are valid and more useful than forcing one overall winner.
Kimi K3, Sol, and Fable may each author pairs only through approved authenticated
routes. A model's view of its own artifact is diagnostic, never binding. These
studies calibrate Robert's taste and BWM process; they are not population-level
proof without repeated projects and raters.

Do not use a blind taste board to certify performance, accessibility, technical
SEO, AEO implementation, analytics, or conversion outcomes. Those require
deterministic/functional evidence; conversion outcomes require observed traffic.

## Truthful operating state

The mechanical pipeline can be operational when source/installed skill hashes,
Brain SHA consumption, schema conformance, validators, and live smoke runs are
proven. Taste weights, premium prediction, learning rates, and cross-project
generalization remain `calibration-pending` until real Robert/client decisions
exist.
