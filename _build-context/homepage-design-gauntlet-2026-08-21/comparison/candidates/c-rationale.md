# Relay Field

## Concept

The page treats the Buildwise relationship as a relay that becomes a living operating rhythm. One important problem enters a clear three-part path, the Pilot is shown as an open aperture rather than a destination, and the final visual expands from one starting point into larger cycles of improvement. Removing the diagrams would remove the distinction between an initial engagement and an ongoing system-building relationship.

## Palette

Deep green gives the page executive weight without defaulting to black. Warm paper keeps it human and editorial. Coral marks the active build phase and Pilot entry; cobalt carries the long-term system; mint and violet distinguish progression. The locked acid-yellow mark remains unchanged and functions as a precise signal within the broader palette.

## Typography

An old-style serif display stack gives the large statements a calm, authored voice. A humanist sans-serif stack keeps navigation, labels, and body copy direct. Both use local/system fallbacks only. Oversized editorial headlines and compact operating labels create hierarchy without technical jargon.

## Composition and visual story

The hero pairs a dominant promise with a smaller working explanation. The transformation section converts that promise into a single connected relay, not a card grid. The Pilot section interrupts the page with an open circular threshold whose path visibly continues. The long-run section resolves the story as a starting node moving through increasingly broad improvement loops, making expansion across the business legible without a fake dashboard.

## Mobile behavior

At 390px, the hero stacks headline, support, and action; the relay becomes one vertical connected line; the Pilot aperture remains visibly open below the copy; and the long-term continuum rotates into a vertical path with progressively larger loops. Type remains at least 17px, actions remain large enough to tap, focus states stay visible, and all visuals are clipped within their sections to prevent horizontal overflow.

## Distinction

This direction avoids the familiar dark-gradient AI aesthetic, circuit imagery, glass cards, invented product UI, and abstract “intelligence” decoration. Its visual language is editorial and operational: color, line, scale, and sequence explain how a practical first problem becomes an enduring company-wide capability.
