# Buildwise Media homepage review NDB

## Decision job

Help an owner decide whether one consequential operational constraint is suitable for a $15,000, 90-day custom-AI Pilot with Buildwise Media.

## Approved direction

Use the locked dark Triangulation marketing register: `#050507` canvas, `#F0FF00` marketing accent, `#B8FF3A` product/success accent, Inter for display and body, and JetBrains Mono for system microtype. Use the Triangulation V3 mark and R014b primitives. The page must feel precise, calm, consequential, and owner-directed—not theatrical, dense, or agency-like.

## Message architecture

The owner chooses one consequential operating constraint. The Buildwise team selects, builds, connects, and tests a custom AI system around that constraint against agreed evidence. Ninety days is the Pilot operating period, not a promised result. The visible message and structured description must retain substance parity.

The homepage must contain exactly these seven sections:

1. Mechanism-Led Hero
2. Choose the Constraint
3. See the System
4. What We Build
5. Evidence and Ownership
6. Pilot Investment
7. Fit Decision

- Primary CTA: `See If We're a Fit` to `/book`.
- Secondary CTA: `See the System` to `#see-the-system`.
- No Robert photo, biography, or founder-led framing.
- Team voice: the Buildwise team plus our AI system.
- No public outcome selection, guarantee, or unsupported ROI, revenue, lead, savings, freedom, or time-return claim.
- The 2026-08-20 homepage authority controls the investment presentation: show price with ownership and evidence context, and do not add an unverified cost-of-assembly or value-stack number from an older homepage rule.
- No retired diagnostic, Revenue Engine, generic automation, agency-package, or software-resale framing.
- Core offer: a custom AI constraint system.
- Bounded enabling work is allowed only when needed for that system and must have a scope exit.
- Open-ended marketing operations, lead-generation responsibility, and revenue accountability are outside this homepage offer.

## Composition and visual behavior

Use one primary idea per section, generous negative space, and a complete no-motion path. Functional diagrams must reveal the mechanism, evidence, or decision path. Decorative atmosphere cannot substitute for a real visual. Keep motion optional and respect reduced-motion. The layout must remain legible and decision-complete at 390px.

## Evidence posture

Material claims are limited to sourced offer facts, process facts, and agreement-bounded ownership language. Use illustrative constraint categories only as examples, never as universal promises. State that rights in client materials, data, and output are governed by the applicable signed agreement.
