# BWM homepage triangulation — Round 5

Status: private taste-lab material only. Not production copy or approval evidence.

## Why the process changes here

Robert supplied enough component-level feedback in Round 4 to end broad blind testing. Round 5 triangulates the selected parts into one direction and tests only the unresolved yellow and blue section language.

## Locked from Robert's feedback

- Use the selected Visual A structure: clean, colorful, simple, readable, strong hierarchy, and clear eye guidance.
- Lock the Round 4 C hero headline: `Build the systems your business needs next.`
- Lock the Round 4 C support: `Buildwise works with you to build and improve custom AI systems across the business. We start with one important problem, learn from real work, and keep building from there.`
- Remove `owner-led service businesses` from the human hero. Robert said it makes the company feel too small.
- Keep the yellow step `Pick one problem that matters.`
- Keep the yellow step `We build and test it.`
- Keep the blue kicker `Built for the long run.`
- The long-term story is continuing to build and improve systems across the business as it becomes AI-native.
- Keep the current Buildwise Media logo unchanged. The text treatment in the taste lab header is not a proposed replacement.

## Explicit rejections

- No `one business, a growing system`; that is not the transformation BWM delivers and sounds complex.
- No `more as you grow`; BWM does not wait for the client to grow before continuing.
- No `start focused`; focus is a standing operating quality, not a phase or differentiator.
- No `add more later`; it sounds deferred and agency-like.
- No `after the first system`, `your business will change`, or `as your business changes` framing.
- No exit-first language such as `run it yourself`, `stop when it doesn't`, or `take what you own`. Ownership belongs elsewhere; it should not replace the ongoing transformation story.
- Do not make continued work sound labor-intensive for the client.

## Blue subheadline triangulation

Robert rejected the locked blue support after evaluating it separately:

- Rejected: `We keep building and improving the systems with you, making AI part of how the whole business works—not another tool sitting off to the side.`
- Reason carried into the next test: it repeats the headline, runs long, and ends in a generic contrast.

The kicker and headline remain locked. Only the blue support is reopened.

- A — ongoing partnership: `We keep building with you—improving each system and bringing AI into more of the work across your business.`
- B — connected system: `The systems build on each other, with our team connecting and improving them across the business.`
- C — continued expansion: `Buildwise keeps improving what is already working and building new systems for more of your business.`

All three options use plain language, preserve Buildwise as the continuing builder, avoid Pilot-first framing, and make no guaranteed outcome claim.
