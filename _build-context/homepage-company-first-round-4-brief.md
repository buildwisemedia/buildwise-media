# BWM homepage blind positioning reset — Round 4

Status: private taste-lab material only. Not production copy or approval evidence.

## Why this round exists

Robert rejected the Round 3 direction because it made the 90-day Pilot sound like the whole company. It implied that Buildwise fixes one problem and then the relationship ends.

That is the wrong hierarchy.

- Buildwise Media's identity: an ongoing partner that builds and improves custom AI systems for owner-led service businesses.
- The Pilot's job: a focused, paid way to begin the relationship with one meaningful operational problem.
- The longer arc: learn from real work, improve the first system, and expand into more of the business when useful.

## Source facts

- Buildwise Media builds custom AI systems for owner-led service businesses.
- The public entry offer is a $15,000, 90-day Pilot.
- The owner chooses the first meaningful operational problem.
- Buildwise builds, connects, and tests the first system with real work.
- The Pilot is the starting engagement, not the full identity or a one-and-done fix.
- Recurring Buildwise relationships exist after the Pilot; do not state recurring prices in this taste round.
- Clients own the system, data, and code within the precise service boundary.
- No business result is guaranteed.

## What each candidate must communicate

1. The hero sells the enduring company-level relationship, not the Pilot.
2. The next section shows how Buildwise can keep improving and expanding the system as the business changes.
3. The Pilot appears only as the practical way to start.
4. The final blue section makes the continued relationship clear without implying dependency or a forced retainer.

## Copy limits

- 5th–7th grade reading level.
- Hero headline: 10 words maximum.
- Hero support: 34 words maximum.
- Growth-section headline: 8 words maximum.
- Growth steps: three, 6 words maximum each.
- Pilot line: 12 words maximum.
- Blue headline: 9 words maximum.
- Blue support: 28 words maximum.
- Use plain words. Avoid `constraint`, `transformation`, `operating layer`, `ecosystem`, `leverage`, and consultant language.
- Do not use generic agency, lead-generation, marketing-package, or software-resale framing.
- Do not invent examples, proof, outcomes, or buyer quotes.
