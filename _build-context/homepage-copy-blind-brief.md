# BWM homepage blind-copy brief

Status: private taste lab only. Not production copy.

Write one concise homepage message direction for Buildwise Media. The reader is an owner of a service business who knows work keeps getting stuck but may not know what kind of AI system would help.

## Facts you may use

- Buildwise Media builds custom AI systems for owner-led service businesses.
- The owner chooses one important operating problem.
- Buildwise builds, connects, and tests a custom AI system around that problem.
- The paid Pilot runs for 90 days.
- The Pilot costs $15,000 paid up front.
- The team and owner review what happened with real work.
- The Pilot does not promise a business result.
- The only permitted problem examples are: requests waiting for the right person; decisions waiting for missing context; and a status being rebuilt by hand.
- Primary CTA: `See If We're a Fit` linking to `/book`.
- Secondary CTA: `See the System` linking to the on-page mechanism.

## Hard exclusions

- No marketing-agency, lead-generation, Revenue Engine, Revenue Leak Map, generic automation, SaaS-resale, or founder-led framing.
- No invented testimonials, customer language, statistics, guarantees, outcomes, urgency, or scarcity.
- Do not invent any example beyond the three permitted problem examples above.
- No vendor names or model names.
- Do not use the phrase `Choose the constraint`.
- Do not use jargon such as leverage, optimize, ecosystem, transformation, omnichannel, scalable solution, or AI-powered magic.

## Voice and reading rules

- Clear over clever. Direct, human, grounded, and operational.
- Target grades 5–7.
- Short sentences and short paragraphs.
- Make the problem concrete enough to recognize in daily work.
- Say what Buildwise does before explaining how the Pilot works.
- Keep reader agency visible.

## Return exactly these fields

1. `eyebrow` — 3–8 words.
2. `headline` — no more than 14 words.
3. `support` — 28–48 words.
4. `mechanism_title` — no more than 9 words.
5. `mechanism_steps` — exactly three steps, each 2–5 words.
6. `fit_title` — no more than 10 words.
7. `fit_support` — 18–32 words.

Return valid JSON only. Do not add commentary, rationale, or markdown.
