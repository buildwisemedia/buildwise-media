Step 1. You are the system.

Every call, quote, and follow-up runs through you. If you don't do it, it doesn't happen.

What it costs you: Missed calls turn into another company's jobs. Your nights go to paperwork. There are no more hours left to give.

Step 2. You bought tools. You're still the glue.

There's a CRM. A scheduling app. Maybe a chatbot. Each one helps a little.

What it costs you: Ten logins that don't talk to each other. Leads still slip through the cracks between them. You didn't get out of the work. You became the manager of the tools.

Why people get stuck here: Every problem gets answered with tool number eleven. More tools means more glue. And the glue is you.
