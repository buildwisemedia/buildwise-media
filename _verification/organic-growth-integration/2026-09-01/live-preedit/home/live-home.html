Real answers.

  • What does Buildwise Media actually do? +

    We install and run an 8-layer revenue operating system for your service business — visibility, conversion, speed-to-lead, sales ops, reputation, paid media, reporting, and strategy operated as one engine. The website is the visible layer. Behind it, our AI system runs the marketing operation 24/7 with quality review appropriate to the work. We treat marketing as operations: investment in, qualified revenue out, verified weekly.

  • Who is Buildwise Media for? +

    Service-business owners who are already generating real revenue and want to stop running their marketing on hope. Owners who want a system that runs whether they watch it or not tend to thrive. Owners looking for a savior, or hoping for comfort, tend to bounce off our process — and that is the right outcome for both of us.

  • How fast does the system go live? +

    Inside 30 days the system is live, the dashboard is open, and the first proof sprint is aimed at the leak with the fastest path to cash. The full install runs through the 45-day proof window.

  • How do you measure success? +

    We start with a written baseline. From there, the dashboard tracks spend, source, follow-up, booked work, and the next move. The signed work order defines the targets, measurement window, and attribution model.

  • Are performance targets guaranteed? +

    No. Targets guide the work and are measured against the captured baseline, but business results depend on factors outside any one system. The agreement defines what is measured and how; it does not guarantee a particular result.

  • Is there a long-term contract? +

    No long-term lock-in. The paid Pilot runs 45 days. The 90-day initial operating period starts on the Ascend handoff. After Day 90, the system is true month-to-month with no penalty, no clawback, no exit fee. We earn the operating fee every month or we do not.

  • Where do you operate? +

    We serve service businesses across the United States. The system installs the same way regardless of metro — local-search depth gets tuned to your specific market during install. Our team is based in Atlanta and we travel to clients on the install when on-site work moves the build forward faster.

  • How do I own my data? +

    Everything we build, you own. The code lives in your repository. The customer list lives in your follow-up system. The ad accounts are in your business. The custom AI agents are configured against your workflows. If you leave, you take the whole system. We walk empty-handed. That ownership is in the agreement as a locked clause.
