Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

1. Who we are

Buildwise Media LLC ("Buildwise Media," "we," "us") operates buildwisemedia.com and builds custom AI systems for owner-led businesses. We're based in Atlanta, Georgia. Our company contact email is hello@buildwisemedia.com.

2. What we collect

When you visit buildwisemedia.com we collect:

  • Usage data — pages visited, referrer, device type, approximate location (city/region from IP), interaction events. Collected via Google Analytics 4, Google Tag Manager, and Microsoft Clarity.
  • Advertising data — when you click an ad, basic conversion signals are collected via Meta Pixel and Meta Conversions API to measure campaign effectiveness.
  • Form data — when you send us a message through our fit or contact forms, we collect the information you provide, such as your name, work email, company, selected interest, request type, and the business issue you describe. We also retain source and campaign details attached to the visit. If you separately schedule through book.buildwisemedia.com, Cal.com processes the booking details you provide there.
  • Text messaging data — when you use our SMS consent form or text our business number, we collect your mobile number, opt-in or opt-out status, consent record, message content, attachments or voice notes you send, delivery records, and related project-routing information.
  • Cookies — first-party cookies for attribution (_bwm_attribution) and third-party cookies set by the analytics tools above.

3. Sub-processors

We share limited data with the following sub-processors strictly to operate the website and conduct outreach:

  • Google LLC (Google Analytics 4, Google Tag Manager) — analytics infrastructure
  • Microsoft Corporation (Microsoft Clarity) — session replay and heatmap analytics
  • Meta Platforms, Inc. (Meta Pixel, Meta Conversions API) — advertising attribution
  • Cloudflare, Inc. — content delivery, hosting, DDoS protection
  • Cal.com, Inc. — appointment scheduling on book.buildwisemedia.com
  • Resend, Inc. — transactional email delivery for form and operational notifications
  • Supabase, Inc. — backend data storage for CRM and operational logs
  • Twilio Inc. and telecommunications carriers — SMS and MMS transport, delivery, and messaging compliance
  • Anthropic, PBC (Claude API) — AI-assisted content production internal to BWM operations

Each sub-processor is contractually obligated to protect data per its own privacy policy and applicable data protection law.

4. How we use it

We use the data above to: operate and improve the website; measure campaign performance; review and reply to fit or contact requests; provide authorized client support and project updates by text; keep consent and delivery records; protect the messaging channel; and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell personal data, ever.

5. Text messaging privacy

We do not share, sell, rent, or otherwise provide your mobile phone number to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are excluded from the other data-sharing categories in this policy and will not be shared with third parties. Service providers acting only on our behalf may process text messaging data as needed to operate messaging, compliance, security, storage, transcription, or customer support; they may not use it for their own marketing or promotional purposes. SMS consent is specific to Buildwise Media and is not transferable to another business.

The Buildwise Media client-support messaging program sends recurring one-to-one customer-care and project-status SMS/MMS messages to invited clients who opt in. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe, START to resubscribe, or HELP for help. See our Terms of Service and SMS consent page.

6. Your rights (GDPR, CCPA)

If you're in the EU/UK or California, you have the right to access, correct, delete, port, or object to the processing of your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, email hello@buildwisemedia.com with the subject line "Data Request." We respond within 30 days.

The Bob pages honor your browser’s Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal by leaving analytics and advertising tags off. Clearing cookies removes stored choices but does not itself stop future tracking. You can also use your browser’s tracking controls.

7. Retention

Google Analytics event and user data retention is set to 14 months. Form submissions and scheduling records may be retained for up to 7 years for business, security, and legal recordkeeping. You can request earlier deletion via the contact above, subject to legal and security exceptions.

We retain text messaging consent, opt-out, and delivery records as long as reasonably needed to operate the program, honor consent choices, resolve disputes, and meet legal or carrier requirements. Message content, attachments, voice notes, and project-routing records are retained according to the applicable client agreement and our operational, security, and legal needs. You may request deletion, subject to legal, contractual, security, and opt-out-record exceptions.

8. Changes to this policy

We update this policy when our data practices change. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
